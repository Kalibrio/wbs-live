Frequently Asked Questions

HOW CAN I SEE THE WORKS IN PERSON?

The studio is currently exhibiting in Paris at 96 Avenue Kléber, 75116 Paris. Private viewings are arranged by appointment — please use the contact form and we will propose a time.

ARE THE WORKS ORIGINAL?

Yes. Every painting on this site is an original, unique work — there are no editions or reproductions. Each work is signed and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity.

WHAT IS YOUR SHIPPING POLICY?

Originals ship from Paris, professionally packed and fully insured, with tracking provided as soon as the work is dispatched.

  • Shipping within France is free.
  • For delivery within the European Union and internationally, please contact us for a quote before purchase — we will confirm packing, carrier, insurance, and timing for your destination.
  • Import duties and taxes, where applicable, are the responsibility of the buyer.

Collectors in Paris are welcome to collect a work in person at the exhibition space by appointment.

WHAT IS YOUR POLICY ON RETURNS?

Sales of original works are final.

Every work is packed to professional standards and insured in transit. In the rare event that a work arrives damaged, please keep all packaging and contact us within 48 hours with photographs — we will handle the insurance process and agree on a resolution with you directly.

HOW DOES THE 30% CHARITY COMMITMENT WORK?

30% of every purchase supports charities dedicated to empowering women and children. The contribution is made by the studio after each sale, and we are happy to share details of the organisations supported — just ask via the contact form.

HOW CAN I KEEP UP WITH YOUR NEW WORK?

Sign up for the newsletter at the bottom of any page to receive new works, studio notes, and private-view invitations. You can also follow the studio on Instagram and Facebook.
