Dog, 2026

Acrylic on canvas
130 × 97 cm / 51 × 38 in
Original, unique work
Available · €3,000 / US$3,500
Signed · Certificate of authenticity included

Rendered in faceted planes of russet, copper, cream, and cool gray against a field of warm rose, Dog gives the Shiba the bearing of a sentinel. The ears are raised, the dark eyes steady, the head turned just enough to keep watch over something beyond the frame — attention without tension, alertness carried lightly.

The Shibuya Dog stands as a silent guardian — watchful, loyal, and unwavering. Like the famous dog of Shibuya station, who returned every day to wait long after waiting had any reason, it embodies a fidelity that does not calculate. Positioned between vulnerability and the unknown, it offers protection not through force, but through presence.

In an era where intelligence is increasingly measured in speed and scale, the guardian represents what cannot be optimized: constancy. Machines may predict, but they do not keep watch the way a dog keeps watch — for someone, not something. Here to observe. Here to endure. Here to protect.
