Robot

$3,500.00
Size: 100 × 100 cm / 39 × 39 in
ENQUIRE TO PURCHASE

A synthetic geometric figure stands at the frontier between humanity and machine intelligence, reduced to its essential architectural forms. Built from large angular planes in ultramarine, teal, muted black, and warm cream, the composition evokes a quiet but unresolved tension: in a world increasingly shaped by AI and autonomous systems, what remains uniquely human?

The absence of detail becomes the message itself. The figure is neither fully human nor fully robotic, but suspended between the two — an abstract reflection on identity, purpose, and consciousness in an AI-led society. Through radical reduction and geometric balance, the painting asks a central question of the coming era: if machines optimize, predict, and execute, what becomes the role of the human?
